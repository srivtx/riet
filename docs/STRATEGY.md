# RIET website: the thinking

## What was wrong
A full-screen CBSE popup, a chat popup, a side tab and a video preloader all arrive before the first sentence. Every page repeats the whole home page. Counters show "0+" or claim figures that differ from the CBSE disclosure. Logo is 210px wide. There are no pages for admissions or academics.

## How parents decide, and what we built for it
| Research finding | On the site |
|---|---|
| Teaching and learning is the top factor, then discipline and safety ([Azim Premji Foundation study](https://www.ideasforindia.in/topics/social-identity/school-choice-in-rural-india-perception-versus-reality.html)) | "Four things every parent asks" directly after the hero |
| Quality and reputation lead urban choice ([NCR study](https://research.jgu.edu.in/whats-best-for-my-kids-an-empirical-assessment-of-primary-school-selection-by-parents-in-urban-india/)) | Evidence instead of adjectives: the CBSE letter itself, declared figures, videos of teachers |
| Informal social networks influence choice ([Lucknow study](https://www.emeraldinsight.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/AEDS-02-2023-0015/full/html)) | WhatsApp is the main action, easy to forward |
| Pre-primary and extra-curricular life draw Odisha parents ([rural Odisha study](https://communities.springernature.com/posts/school-choice-in-rural-odisha-india-understanding-from-a-multi-stakeholder-perspective)) | Early stages first; "A year under the RIET sun" |

No survey specific to Bhubaneswar parents turned up. Local judgement here is ours; check it with real parents.

Principles used: proof beats claims; one small commitment first (the three-tap sentence); faces before buildings; pride in Odisha; no popups, no false urgency.

## Competitors seen
SAI: pale, serif, posed studio children, brochure form. ODM: group portal of logos. KiiT: scholarship popup, corporate blue. RIET's opening: real children, warmth, closeness to home, Odia identity.

## Honesty rules
- CBSE affiliation 1530488 is for Thakurpatna only (the letter says so). Never state it for other campuses.
- Numbers come from the mandatory disclosure, not the old counters.
- No invented results, rankings, testimonials or timings.
